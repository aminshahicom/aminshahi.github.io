Founder

Graphic Designers Club of Isfahan.

I founded the Graphic Designers Club of Isfahan with the vision of building a connected and active community for young graphic designers and creative professionals.

The initiative was created to strengthen the local design ecosystem by bringing together emerging talents, encouraging collaboration, creating opportunities for professional exchange, and expanding the role of graphic design in the city’s cultural and creative landscape.

Beyond establishing a community, my goal was to create a platform for collective growth, professional engagement, and the development of a stronger design culture in Isfahan.
